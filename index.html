FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is BrightNest a real cleaning company?

No. BrightNest is a fictional company created for this portfolio demo. Nothing on this page books, charges or contacts a real cleaner.

Are the prices real?

No — they are illustrative examples ($129 / $189 / $289 one-time). The formula is in js/config.js so you can see exactly how each quote is calculated, and change it in one place.

Are the reviews real?

No. All three are samples with invented names. A real business must use genuine reviews with permission, and must not invent a star rating.

What happens when I submit a form?

Nothing is sent or stored. The form is validated in your browser and shows you a local summary you can copy or download. There is no backend, CRM, email service or payment in this demo — see the README for what a live version needs.

Do the phone, email and WhatsApp links work?

Not in demo mode. They are labelled DEMO and, when clicked, explain that they are placeholders. Set isDemo: false in js/config.js with real, verified details to enable them.

Which areas would a real version serve?

The demo uses Austin, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville and Lakeway as sample areas. A real business should list only the areas it actually serves.
